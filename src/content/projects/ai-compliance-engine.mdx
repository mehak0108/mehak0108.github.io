## Problem

JP Morgan's compliance team reviewed thousands of transactions manually each week. A 4-day batch process meant that potential violations were caught days late — by which point regulatory exposure had already accumulated.

## Solution

I led product for an ML-powered compliance automation engine that:

- Auto-classified transactions by risk tier using a trained XGBoost model
- Surfaced edge cases to analyst review queues with reasoning explanations
- Integrated with existing JIRA-based workflows — no retraining required

The model was trained on 18 months of historical review decisions, with feedback loops built in from day one.

## Outcome

Shipped to 3 business lines within 8 months. The team went from 4-day batch reviews to near-real-time flagging, false positives dropped 28%, and analyst time on routine cases fell by 40%.
